# v0.2 — Walking, physical objects and streamed terrain

This release advances M2/M3 with a complete playable slice. The native Android app
still renders through ash/direct Vulkan. It opens beside a stack of movable voxel
crates, with walking, gravity, jumping, terrain edits and grab/throw/break controls.
Flight remains available for inspecting the world; HOME returns to a clear spawn.

## Delivered contracts

- Greedy chunk meshes reuse block-mesh 0.2.0. Only edited chunks and affected
  neighbors rebuild; the Vulkan renderer retains other buffers and culls chunks
  against the camera frustum. The reference exposed-face mesher remains available.
- Terrain extends across a bounded 512 by 512 voxel domain. A synchronous 7 by 7
  by 3 chunk window keeps residency bounded. Saved chunk overrides survive eviction
  and revisits. Existing v0.1 terrain, including removed chunks, remains authoritative.
- Rapier 0.32.0 supplies the fixed 60 Hz solver, capsule controller, rigid bodies,
  shape queries and spring constraint. Terrain colliders derive from authoritative
  solid voxels, independently of the render mesh. Edits publish collision before
  the next simulation step; there are no asynchronous jobs to resurrect stale data.
- Objects are solid volumes of half-meter voxels. BREAK separates a one-meter
  crate into its eight physical voxels, preserving material, volume and mass.
  Density-based collider mass/inertia and bounded impulses are described in the
  [physics contract](../crates/matterweave-physics/README.md). At most 64 bodies exist.
- Distant objects stop simulating before their terrain leaves residency and resume
  on return. Object bodies, velocities, camera and terrain share one atomic save.
  Active grab constraints are released on lifecycle transitions and are not saved.
- The development profile now uses Rust optimization level 2, retaining debug
  assertions and the same debug signing key for the published APK upgrade.

## Touch controls

Drag MOVE for walking and free space for looking. JUMP clears a one-voxel step.
Aim at the colored crates and use GRAB/DROP, THROW or BREAK. REMOVE/PLACE edit terrain;
placement overlapping the walking character is refused. WALK/FLY toggles flight,
where UP/DOWN control elevation. HOME releases a held object and returns to spawn.
RESET OBJECTS rebuilds the demo stack while preserving terrain edits. SAVE retries persistence, SWAP changes the movement side, and SIZE enlarges the pad.
Edits save immediately; ongoing camera/object state saves periodically and on pause.

Keyboard controls are WASD, Space (jump/up), Shift (flight down), right-drag (look),
Q/left-click (remove), E (place), G (grab/drop), T (throw), B (break), F (walk/fly),
Home (spawn), F5 (save), F6 (reset objects), H (swap), J (size).

## Remaining milestone gates

This is not the complete M2/M3 acceptance report. The equivalent-quality mobile
ray/mesh/hybrid renderer comparison, asynchronous streaming pressure/cancellation,
large destruction workloads and broad device coverage remain outstanding. Streaming
and collision preparation are synchronous and can cause boundary-crossing stalls.
The save cap is 512 authoritative chunk overrides and 12 MiB total; new edits are
refused at the cap while existing overridden chunks remain editable.

Lighting remains the direct sun/ambient/fog baseline. Direct shadows, indirect
illumination, reflections, automatic fine-detail LOD and smooth LOD transitions are
not included. These remain M4 work; no Lumen/Nanite equivalence is claimed.
