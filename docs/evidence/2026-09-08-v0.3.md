## Executed verification

All 77 Rust tests pass: 29 core, 17 explorer, 17 physics and 14 renderer.
Workspace formatting and Clippy pass. The unchanged vendored winit X11 cast
warning remains. The coordination board's 16 ownership/recovery tests pass.
Host app smoke presented 90 frames with edits, grab/throw/fracture, atomic
save/reload, resize and renderer recreation under Vulkan synchronization validation.
Renderer smoke presented ten frames covering shadow off/on/off, 1024/2048 maps,
offscreen casters, resize/zero extent/recreation, stale/invalid uploads and teardown.
These host runs used llvmpipe; their timings are not mobile measurements.

On the phone, upgrading retained saved terrain, camera and 11 old bodies.
Reset creates six arch objects; fracturing the beam changed six bodies to 29.
Shadow, sun and detail controls respond. An isolated exposed cube-face rectangle
(1000, 400)-(1400, 650) is RGB-identical with shadows on/off, including the final
hardware-PCF path at low sun/2048. Complex stepped-terrain shadow edges remain
coarse: one 128 m map gives 12.5 cm texels at 1024 and 6.25 cm at 2048.
An explicit depth-fetch diagnostic did not improve this and was reverted.

## Sustained capture

measurement, fixed saved seed 20260907,
11 bodies, camera (12, 4.517111, 23), default sun and 1024 shadows, no adaptive quality.
The phone was USB powered and already hot after interactive use. Ambient/case
conditions were not measured. Earlier interactive v0.3 CSV is excluded. App logs
contain no actions and retain the same camera, 11 bodies and process throughout.

All 61,509 selected presentation intervals were co-observed in compositor dumps.
Their span is 1200.339 seconds; first-observation boundaries are approximate.
Mean/median/p95/p99 were 19.514846/16.584532/24.877500/24.879270 ms;
maximum 33.167344 ms. This is observed presentation cadence, not input latency.
There were no unsupported bridges, failed dumps or malformed timestamp rows.
One dump's newest timestamp regressed; global deduplication/co-observation rules
retain the evidence without treating dump order as presentation order.

The 61,492 approximately aligned CSV rows had mean main-loop wall time 19.500775 ms,
mesh preparation/upload wall time 2.695324 ms, completed GPU interval 2.290743 ms
and shadow interval 0.256010 ms. GPU records refer to earlier submissions and the
render interval can include image-acquire semaphore waits. These are not additive
CPU/GPU busy times or a same-frame presentation join. CSV alignment has about
1.012 seconds known boundary uncertainty plus unmeasured initialization delay/drift.
Stationary streaming-request cost does not measure active streaming performance.

All 40 measurement health samples reported severe throttling (status 3).
Battery temperature was 42.9→45.1°C, maximum 45.3°C; reported skin temperature
54.198→56.515°C. Battery level fell 58→52% despite USB power; this is not an
energy measurement. Process PSS ranged 54,788–145,313 KB with graphics-accounting
dips; similar endpoints do not establish memory stability. The earlier v0.2 run
started cooler and lacked the CSV overhead, so neither speed nor power superiority

## Limits

This implements direct shadows and bounded background preparation, not GI,
reflections, automatic LOD or thermal adaptation. Collision publication, uploads,
snapshot copying and saves still include synchronous work. The equivalent-quality
ray/mesh/hybrid comparison remains deferred. No mobile Vulkan validation layer,
physical simultaneous multi-finger test or second-device coverage was available.
