## Scope of the evidence

This checks the *additional recorder/stage instrumentation* on this short,
stationary workload. Existing GPU timestamp bookkeeping remains enabled in both
modes. Its own overhead, pure GPU on/off overhead, sustained20min behavior, input
latency, streaming/edit/collision workload repeatability and full P01 acceptance
remain unqualified. Ambient/case were unobserved. Three pairs are not a confidence
interval or a universal performance guarantee. No P02 optimization or energy win
is claimed.

`overhead-off-01` is excluded: it sampled `/proc/PID/task/PID/stat`, the Android UI
task, not the native engine thread. Corrected runs use whole-process
`/proc/PID/stat`. The first matched-readiness recipe occasionally undershot120s
because of timing precision; failed observations were retained. Sampling spacing
became30.05s without weakening the validator.
